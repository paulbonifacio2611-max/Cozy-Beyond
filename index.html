About us

Our story, mission, and vision

History

HISTORY

“The co-founders of Cozy & Beyond had a vision of creating a welcoming space where different people could enjoy good food, have a relaxing time, and a unique cultural experience. The café was established to bring the forgotten Filipino culture into a modern and comfortable setting.

The founders envisioned a refuge that would go beyond simply serving food and beverages. They wanted to create a place where customers could experience Filipino traditions through the ambience, designs, and the hospitality of the infrastructure. The Café’s concept draws inspiration from the strong sense of craftsmanship and connection of the oldest society of the Philippines.

The foundation focused on the accessibility, creativity, and efficient service of our establishment. Today, the café continues its vision by combining our cultural identity with different innovations and creations.”

Mission

MISSION

“We strive to deliver friendly, efficient service while creating a place where everyone can relax and be able to have a connection with the spirits of our ancestors. At Cozy & Beyond café, we bring warmth and nostalgia with every sip and bite. Through every interaction, we hope to give each customer a meaningful and memorable experience.”

Vision

VISION

“To become an establishment where we preserve the hidden beauty and heritage of the Philippines by bringing traditional Filipino culture, flavors, and craftsmanship into a modern and creative experience.

Cozy & Beyond aims to inspire people to appreciate the beauty of our past while learning, creating meaningful connections, and sharing memorable experiences for everyone.”

Goals

BUSINESS GOALS

“Cozy & Beyond honors pre-colonial Filipino culture by serving unique food and drinks that blend our rich history with modern flavors, all made from local ingredients. Step into our café and experience heritage through curated antiques and design elements. Our goal is to provide exceptional service and a memorable cultural experience where guests of all ages can relax and feel connected to our roots.”
